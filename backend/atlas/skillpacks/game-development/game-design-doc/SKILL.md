Use this to write a game design document.

## Steps
1. Pitch: one sentence, the genre, the platform and the target player.
2. Core loop: what the player does again and again, in one sentence, and why it's fun.
3. Mechanics: controls, rules, systems and how they interact.
4. Progression: goals, rewards, difficulty curve, and the economy if there is one.
5. Content: levels or areas, enemies, items, and story beats.
6. Art and sound direction with reference images. Scope: an MVP feature list, risks and milestones.

## Watch out for
- Huge documents nobody reads. Keep it short and alive.
- Skipping the prototype. Prove the core loop is fun before writing more.

## Output
The GDD in Markdown with those sections.
