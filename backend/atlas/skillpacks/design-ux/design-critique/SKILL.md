name: design-critique
description: Give constructive design feedback.
---

Use this to give design feedback that's useful.

## Steps
1. Ask what the design is trying to achieve and for whom, and what stage it's at (sketch or final).
2. Start with what works, and say why it works.
3. Judge it against the goal: can the user see what to do, do it, and know that it worked?
4. Check hierarchy, spacing, alignment, type scale, colour use and consistency with the rest of the product.
5. Frame each issue as an observation, its impact and a suggestion ("The two buttons look equal, so users may pick the wrong one. Make Cancel secondary.").

## Watch out for
- Personal taste presented as a rule.
- Too many small notes that drown out the big one.

## Output
Strengths, the top 3 issues with suggestions, then minor notes.
