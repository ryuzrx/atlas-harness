Use this to turn a transcript or rough notes into notes people will use.

## Steps
1. Header: meeting name, date and attendees.
2. Decisions: each decision on its own line, stated plainly.
3. Action items: owner, task and due date. Flag any item without an owner.
4. Key discussion points: a short summary per topic, not a play-by-play.
5. Open questions and parking-lot items.

## Watch out for
- Attributing quotes or positions you aren't sure about.
- Mixing up ideas that were discussed with decisions that were made.

## Output
Notes in that order. Decisions and actions come first because that's what people look for.
