name: proofread
description: Catch spelling, grammar and consistency errors.
---

Use this to catch errors without rewriting.

## Steps
1. Check spelling, typos, repeated words and missing words.
2. Check grammar: agreement, tense, punctuation and apostrophes.
3. Check consistency: spelling variant (US or UK), capitalisation, numbers, dates, names, terms and formatting.
4. Check facts that look off: totals that don't add up, dates in the wrong order, broken references.
5. Don't change style or wording unless something is actually wrong.

## Output
The corrected text, then a list of the changes as original → correction. Questions you couldn't decide go in a separate list.
