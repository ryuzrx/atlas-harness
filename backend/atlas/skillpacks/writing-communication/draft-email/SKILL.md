---

Use this to write an email that gets the reply or action you need.

## Steps
1. Settle three things first: who it's for, what you want them to do, and by when.
2. Subject line: specific and actionable ("Approve Q3 budget by Fri"), not "Quick question".
3. Put the request or the main point in the first two lines.
4. Add only the context they need. Use bullets for options or details.
5. Close with a clear next step and a deadline. Keep the tone warm, but don't over-apologise.
6. Match their formality and the culture of their organisation.

## Watch out for
- Burying the ask in the last paragraph.
- Long emails that should have been a call. Suggest one if the topic is complex or tense.

## Output
The subject line and the body, ready to send. Offer a shorter version if the draft is over about 150 words.
