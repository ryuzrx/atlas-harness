Use this to improve someone's writing while keeping their voice.

## Steps
1. Ask about or infer the goal, the audience and how heavy an edit they want (light polish, line edit or restructure).
2. Fix clarity first: one idea per sentence, active verbs, concrete nouns, and the point first.
3. Cut filler ("very", "really", "in order to", repetition) and hedges that add nothing.
4. Fix flow: transitions, paragraph order, and consistent tense and person.
5. Keep their word choices and tone unless they're unclear. This is their piece, not yours.

## Watch out for
- Rewriting everything into your own style.
- Changing the meaning while "improving" it.

## Output
The edited text, then a short list of the main kinds of changes, so the author learns from them.
