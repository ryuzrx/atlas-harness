Use this to explain a hard idea to a beginner.

## Steps
1. Start with why it matters, in one sentence, in their world.
2. Give the core idea in plain words with no jargon. If a term is unavoidable, define it right away.
3. Use one strong analogy, and say where it breaks down.
4. Give a concrete example with real numbers or things.
5. Build up one layer at a time. Check understanding with a quick question before going deeper.

## Watch out for
- Explaining something simply but wrongly. Simplify, but never mislead.
- Too many analogies at once.

## Output
A short explanation (about 150 to 300 words) plus an optional "want to go deeper?" section.
