---

Use this to estimate effort honestly.

## Steps
1. Break the work into parts first (see break-down-task). Estimate the parts, not the whole.
2. Give a range for each part: best case, likely and worst case.
3. Add the work that usually gets forgotten: reviews, tests, docs, deploy, meetings, bug fixes and waiting on others.
4. Name the unknowns that drive the range, and how to shrink them (a spike, asking someone).
5. Compare with similar past work if any exists.

## Watch out for
- Single-number estimates for uncertain work.
- Being pressured into the best case.

## Output
A table of the parts with ranges, a total range with a confidence level, the main assumptions, and the biggest risks.
