description: Run a useful retrospective.
---

Use this to run or write up a retrospective that leads to change.

## Steps
1. Set the scope: which sprint, project or incident, and its goal.
2. Gather facts first: a timeline, key events, and metrics.
3. Collect what went well, what didn't, and what puzzled people. Focus on the system, not on blaming people.
4. Group them into themes and pick the top 2 or 3 that matter most.
5. For each theme, agree on one specific action with an owner and a date.
6. Check the actions from the last retro: were they done, and did they help?

## Output
A summary, the themes, actions (owner and date), and the status of previous actions.
