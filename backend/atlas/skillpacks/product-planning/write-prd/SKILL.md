Use this to write a short product requirements document.

## Steps
1. Problem: who has it, how we know (evidence), and why now.
2. Goals and non-goals. Make the goals measurable where you can.
3. Users and the main use cases.
4. Requirements: must-have versus nice-to-have, written as behaviour, not UI design.
5. User experience: the key flows, with sketches or links.
6. Success metrics and how they'll be measured.
7. Risks, dependencies, open questions and a rough timeline.

## Watch out for
- Jumping straight to the solution without the problem.
- Requirements no one can test.

## Output
A one-to-two-page document with those sections.
