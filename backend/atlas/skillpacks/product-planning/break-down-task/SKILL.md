---
name: break-down-task
description: Split a big task into small verifiable steps.
---

Use this to split a large piece of work into small steps that can each be checked.

## Steps
1. State what "done" looks like for the whole task in one or two sentences.
2. Work backwards: what has to be true just before done? And before that?
3. Make each step small (half a day or less), independently checkable, and valuable or at least de-risking.
4. Put the riskiest or most uncertain step early, as a spike or prototype if needed.
5. Mark dependencies and steps that can run in parallel.
6. Give each step its own check: a test, a demo or a measurable result.

## Output
A numbered list of steps, each with: what, how to verify, its dependencies, and a rough size. The first step should be something you can start right now.
