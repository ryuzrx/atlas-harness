Use this to get an app ready for App Store or Play Store review.

## Steps
1. Metadata: name, subtitle, description, keywords, screenshots for every required size, and a privacy policy URL.
2. Privacy: privacy labels or the data-safety form matching what the app actually collects, permission prompts that explain why, and ATT on iOS if you track users.
3. Accounts: if users can sign up, they must be able to delete their account in the app. Provide a demo login for reviewers.
4. Payments: digital goods go through in-app purchase, with a working restore-purchases option.
5. Quality: no crashes, no placeholder content, works offline or fails gracefully, and supports the required screen sizes.
6. Build: correct version and build number, signing, and the target SDK or minimum OS required by the store.

## Output
A checklist with each item marked pass, fail or unknown, and the fixes needed.
