Use this to check an invoice for mistakes.

Note: this is a practical check, not accounting or tax advice.

## Steps
1. Check the basics: vendor details, invoice number and date, the due date, and the payment details (watch for changed bank details, which are a common fraud sign).
2. Match it to the purchase order, contract or quote: items, quantities, rates and agreed discounts.
3. Recalculate the line totals, subtotal, tax and grand total.
4. Look for duplicates (same amount or number as an earlier invoice) and charges outside the scope.
5. Check that the tax treatment and required fields fit the jurisdiction.

## Output
Pass or fail, a list of the discrepancies with amounts, and questions for the vendor.
