Use this to organise an expense report.

## Steps
1. Gather the receipts. For each one, record the date, vendor, amount, currency, category and business purpose.
2. Check them against the policy: limits, allowed categories, and required approvals or receipts.
3. Convert currencies using the rate the policy specifies, and note the rate used.
4. Mark missing receipts, duplicates and personal items.
5. Total by category and overall.

## Output
An itemised table, totals, and a list of issues to resolve before submitting.
