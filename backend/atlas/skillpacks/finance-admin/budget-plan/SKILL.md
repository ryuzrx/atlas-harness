Use this to build a simple budget.

Note: this is general information, not professional financial advice. For decisions with big consequences, talk to a qualified adviser.

## Steps
1. List net monthly income from all sources.
2. List fixed costs (rent, bills, debt payments, subscriptions) and average variable costs from the last 2 or 3 months of statements.
3. Pick a framework, such as 50/30/20 (needs / wants / saving and debt) or zero-based, and adjust it to the person's reality.
4. Set goals: an emergency fund, paying down debt, and savings targets with dates.
5. Find savings: unused subscriptions, the biggest variable categories.
6. Plan a short monthly review.

## Output
A budget table (category, planned, actual), goals, and suggested changes.
