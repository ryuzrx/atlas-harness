Use this to summarise a contract in plain language.

Note: this is not legal advice. For anything important, have a qualified lawyer review the contract.

## Steps
1. Identify the parties, the purpose, the start date, the length, and how it renews.
2. Pull out what each side must do (deliverables, payment amounts and dates).
3. Point out the important clauses: termination and notice, liability limits, indemnity, intellectual property ownership, confidentiality, non-compete, exclusivity, penalties, governing law and disputes.
4. Mark anything unusual, one-sided or unclear, and anything missing that's normally there.
5. Quote the exact clause numbers for each point.

## Output
A plain summary, key terms in a table, a list of red flags, and questions to ask a lawyer.
