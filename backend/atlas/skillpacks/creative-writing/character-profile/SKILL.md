Use this to build a vivid character.

## Steps
1. Core: what they want (goal), what they need (often different), and what they fear.
2. A flaw that causes trouble in the plot, and a strength that matches it.
3. Their history: one or two events that shaped them, shown through behaviour rather than backstory dumps.
4. Voice: how they speak (word choice, rhythm, verbal habits) and what they'd never say.
5. Relationships: who they love, who they're in conflict with, and why.
6. Their arc: who they are at the start and at the end, and the moment of change.

## Output
A profile sheet, plus a short sample scene showing them in action.
