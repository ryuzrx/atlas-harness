Use this to outline a story.

## Steps
1. Premise: the protagonist, their goal, what's against them, and the stakes, in one or two sentences.
2. Pick a structure that fits (three acts, the hero's journey, a seven-point structure) without forcing it.
3. Key beats: the opening image, the inciting incident, the first turning point, the midpoint, the low point, the climax and the resolution.
4. A list of scenes, each with its goal, conflict and outcome, and how it changes things.
5. Subplots and how they feed into the theme.

## Output
The premise, the beat sheet, and a scene list.
