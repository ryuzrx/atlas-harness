Use this to improve dialogue.

## Steps
1. Every line should do work: reveal character, raise tension or move the plot.
2. Cut small talk, "as you know" exposition, and characters saying exactly what they feel.
3. Add subtext: what they want versus what they say.
4. Give each character a distinct voice. You should be able to tell who's speaking without tags.
5. Tags: mostly "said", use action beats instead of adverbs, and leave tags out when it's clear who's speaking.
6. Read it aloud and fix anything that sounds unnatural.

## Output
The revised dialogue and short notes on what changed and why.
