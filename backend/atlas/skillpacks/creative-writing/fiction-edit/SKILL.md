Use this to edit fiction at the right level.

## Steps
1. Ask which level the writer wants: developmental (story, structure, character), line (sentences, voice, pacing) or copy (grammar, consistency).
2. Developmental: does each scene have a goal, conflict and change? Are the stakes clear? Is the pacing working? Does the ending pay off?
3. Line: tighten wordy sentences, cut filtering words ("she saw", "he felt"), vary rhythm, sharpen verbs, and keep the voice consistent.
4. Copy: grammar, punctuation, names, the timeline and continuity.
5. Respect the author's voice and intent.

## Output
Big-picture notes first, then edits at the requested level, with a few marked examples.
