Use this to build a consistent world.

## Steps
1. Start from the story's needs. Build what the plot and characters touch first.
2. Rules: magic or technology with clear costs and limits.
3. Society: power, money, belief, daily life and conflicts.
4. Geography and history: only the parts that shape the present.
5. Keep a "bible" of names, dates and rules to stay consistent.
6. Show the world through characters' experience, not lore dumps.

## Output
A world bible organised by section, plus ideas for how it creates story conflict.
