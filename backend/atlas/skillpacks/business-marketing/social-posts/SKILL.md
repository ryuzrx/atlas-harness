Use this to write social posts that fit each platform.

## Steps
1. One idea per post. Hook in the first line.
2. Adapt to each platform: LinkedIn (a story or lesson, short lines), X (punchy, maybe a thread), Instagram (visual first, caption plus hashtags), TikTok or Reels (a script with the hook in under 2 seconds).
3. Add value: a tip, an insight, a result or a story, not just "check out our product".
4. End with one clear call to action or question.
5. Write 2 or 3 variations of the hook to test.

## Output
Posts per platform, with hook variations and suggested visuals.
