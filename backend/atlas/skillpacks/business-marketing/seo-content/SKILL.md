Use this to write content that answers a search.

## Steps
1. Work out the search intent: is the searcher trying to learn, compare or buy? Look at what currently ranks and what's missing from it.
2. Outline it: an H1 with the main term, H2s for the sub-questions people ask, and a direct answer near the top.
3. Write useful, specific content: examples, data and steps. It should be better than the pages that rank today.
4. Include related terms naturally, internal links, and images with alt text.
5. Write the title tag, meta description and URL slug.

## Watch out for
- Padding for word count.
- Keyword stuffing.

## Output
The brief (intent, keywords, outline), then the draft with its meta tags.
