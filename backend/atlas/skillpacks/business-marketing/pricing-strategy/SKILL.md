Use this to think through pricing.

## Steps
1. Understand the value: what outcome customers get, and what they pay for alternatives today.
2. Pick the value metric that grows with how much they get (seats, usage, projects).
3. Set the floor (your costs plus margin) and the ceiling (value and competitors).
4. Design 2 or 3 tiers for clear customer types, with a recommended middle tier.
5. Decide on a free trial or free plan, annual discounts and how you'll show the price.
6. Test with real conversations or experiments. Review again after launch.

## Output
The recommended model, tiers with prices and limits, the reasoning, and what to test.
