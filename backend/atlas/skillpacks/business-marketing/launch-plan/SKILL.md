Use this to plan a product launch.

## Steps
1. Goal: one measurable target (sign-ups, revenue, users who become active) and a date.
2. Audience and message: who it's for, the main promise, and 3 proof points.
3. Before the launch: a waitlist, beta users, testimonials, assets (page, demo video, screenshots), and press or creator outreach.
4. Launch day: the channels (email, social, communities, Product Hunt, partners), a timed schedule, and owners.
5. After the launch: follow-ups, onboarding emails, collecting feedback, and a metrics review after a week.
6. Risks and backups: site load, support volume, and what you'll do if it goes quiet.

## Output
A timeline with tasks, owners, channels and metrics.
