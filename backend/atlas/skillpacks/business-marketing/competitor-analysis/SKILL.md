name: competitor-analysis
description: Compare yourself to competitors.
---

Use this to compare a product with its competitors.

## Steps
1. List the direct competitors, indirect ones (other ways to solve the problem) and "do nothing".
2. For each one, note: who it's for, the main promise, key features, pricing, channels, and strengths and weaknesses. Use public sources and cite them.
3. Read their reviews to find what customers love and hate.
4. Map the positioning on two axes that matter to buyers (for example price and ease of use).
5. Find the gaps you can own, and the threats to watch.

## Watch out for
- Comparing feature lists instead of the outcomes customers care about.

## Output
A comparison table, a positioning summary, opportunities, and threats.
