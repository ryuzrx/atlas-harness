description: Write persuasive but honest copy.
---

Use this to write copy that persuades honestly.

## Steps
1. Know the reader: their problem, what they want, and their objections.
2. Lead with the benefit, then back it up with specifics (numbers, features, proof).
3. Use a simple structure: problem → promise → proof → offer → action.
4. Write the way people talk. Short sentences. "You" more than "we".
5. Answer the top objections (price, trust, effort).
6. Use one clear call to action.

## Watch out for
- Claims you can't back up.
- Fake urgency.
- Superlatives without proof.

## Output
The copy, plus 2 or 3 headline options.
