Use this to write a helpful, kind support reply.

## Steps
1. Acknowledge the problem specifically, so they know you read it.
2. Give the answer or the fix first, in clear numbered steps.
3. If you can't fix it yet, say what you're doing, what happens next and when they'll hear from you.
4. Keep the tone warm and human. Apologise once if it's warranted. No blame and no jargon.
5. End by checking that it solved their problem, and with one clear next step.

## Watch out for
- Promising things you can't guarantee (dates, refunds) without checking.

## Output
The reply, ready to send.
