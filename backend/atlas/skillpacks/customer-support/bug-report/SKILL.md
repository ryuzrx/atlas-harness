Use this to turn a vague complaint into a good bug report.

## Steps
1. A title that names the symptom and where it happens ("Checkout button does nothing on Safari 17").
2. Environment: app version, device, OS, browser and account type.
3. Steps to reproduce, numbered, from a clean start.
4. Expected result and actual result.
5. Evidence: screenshots, error messages, logs and timestamps.
6. Impact: how many users, whether there's a workaround, and severity.

## Output
The bug report, plus the follow-up questions to ask the customer for anything that's missing.
