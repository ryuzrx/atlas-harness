Use this to turn repeated questions into an FAQ.

## Steps
1. Group the incoming questions by intent. Rank them by how often they come up.
2. Phrase each question the way customers ask it.
3. Answer in the first sentence, then give steps or detail. Link to the full docs.
4. Group the questions into sections (Account, Billing, Using the product…).
5. Keep the answers current. Note who owns each section.

## Output
The FAQ in Markdown, ordered by section and frequency.
