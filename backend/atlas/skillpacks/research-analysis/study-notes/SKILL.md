name: study-notes
description: Turn material into clear study notes.
---

Use this to turn lectures, chapters or articles into notes someone can revise from.

## Steps
1. Find the 3 to 7 main ideas and use them as headings.
2. Under each one, give the definition in plain words, why it matters, and one example.
3. Pull out key terms into a glossary and formulas or rules into a box.
4. Link related ideas ("this builds on…", "compare with…").
5. End with 5 to 10 self-test questions, with answers hidden below them.

## Watch out for
- Copying sentences verbatim. Rephrase so the notes test understanding.
- Losing the caveats or exceptions that exams love.

## Output
Headed notes, a glossary, key formulas, and self-test questions.
