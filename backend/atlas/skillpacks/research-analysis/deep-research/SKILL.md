---
name: deep-research
description: Research a question thoroughly and report with sources.
---

Use this for a question that needs several sources and a reasoned answer.

## Steps
1. Restate the question and split it into 3 to 6 sub-questions.
2. For each sub-question, find primary sources first (official docs, papers, data, filings), then reputable secondary ones.
3. Note each source's date and who published it. Prefer recent sources for fast-moving topics.
4. Look for disagreement between sources and explain it rather than picking one silently.
5. Separate what's established, what's likely and what's unknown.
6. Write the answer first, then the supporting detail.

## Watch out for
- Citing a source you didn't actually read.
- Treating one blog post as consensus.
- Stating figures without dates or units.

## Output
A direct answer, key findings with inline citations, open questions, and a source list (title, publisher, date, link).
