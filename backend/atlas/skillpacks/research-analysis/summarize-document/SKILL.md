name: summarize-document
description: Summarise a long document faithfully.
---

Use this to summarise a long document without distorting it.

## Steps
1. Find out who the summary is for and how long it should be. Default: about 10% of the original, and no more than a page.
2. Skim the structure first (headings, abstract, conclusion), then read the full text.
3. Capture the main claim, the supporting points, the evidence, and any limits or caveats the author gives.
4. Keep the author's position separate from your own comments. Mark your comments clearly if you add any.
5. Keep important numbers, names and dates exactly as written.

## Watch out for
- Adding conclusions the document doesn't make.
- Dropping the caveats.
- Over-weighting the opening pages.

## Output
A one-line takeaway, a short summary, key points as bullets, and (if asked) open questions or criticisms in a separate section.
