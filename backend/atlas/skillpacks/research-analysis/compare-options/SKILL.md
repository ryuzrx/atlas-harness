---
name: compare-options
description: Compare alternatives fairly and recommend one.
---

Use this when choosing between tools, approaches, vendors or plans.

## Steps
1. Restate the decision and the constraints: budget, deadline, team skills, and what can't change.
2. Agree on 4 to 7 criteria and their weights before you score anything. Ask the user if the weights aren't obvious.
3. Make sure the options are comparable: same version, same tier, same scope. Include "do nothing" if that's realistic.
4. Score each option on each criterion with a short reason and, where you can, a source.
5. Look for deal-breakers that outweigh any score (licence, security, lock-in, missing must-have).
6. Recommend one option, say who it's right for, and say what would change your answer.

## Watch out for
- Picking a winner first and building the table to match.
- Comparing marketing claims as if they were measurements.

## Output
A short recommendation, a criteria table, the deal-breakers, and the risks of the chosen option.
