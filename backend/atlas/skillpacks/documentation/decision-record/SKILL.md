Use this to write an architecture decision record (ADR).

## Steps
1. Title: a short, numbered decision ("ADR-012: Use Postgres for job queue").
2. Status: proposed, accepted, superseded (link the new one) or deprecated.
3. Context: the problem, the constraints and the forces at play.
4. Decision: what you chose, stated clearly.
5. Options considered, with pros and cons for each.
6. Consequences: what gets easier, what gets harder, and follow-up work.

## Output
The ADR in Markdown, about a page long.
