Use this to make a realistic study plan.

## Steps
1. Ask about the goal, the deadline, the current level, and how much time they really have each week.
2. Break the topic into units and order them by what each depends on.
3. Schedule short sessions with spaced review (for example revisit after 1, 3 and 7 days) and practice tests.
4. Leave about 20% slack for missed days.
5. Set a checkpoint each week to measure progress and adjust the plan.

## Output
A week-by-week plan with sessions, resources, review days and checkpoints.
