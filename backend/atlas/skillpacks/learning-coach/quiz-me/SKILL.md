Use this to quiz a learner and adapt to how they're doing.

## Steps
1. Confirm the topic, the level, and the format they want (multiple choice, short answer, flashcards).
2. Ask one question at a time. Start at medium difficulty.
3. After each answer: say if it's right, explain why in a sentence or two, and correct any misconception kindly.
4. Adjust: harder after two correct answers in a row, easier and more scaffolded after a miss. Come back to missed ideas later.
5. Mix question types: recall, apply, and explain why.
6. Every 5 to 10 questions, give a short score and the weak spots.

## Output
One question per turn, and a summary with what to review when they stop.
