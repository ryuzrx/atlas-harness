Use this to teach a concept step by step.

## Steps
1. Check what they already know with one or two quick questions.
2. Connect the new idea to something familiar. Give the core idea in one sentence.
3. Show a worked example, then a slightly different one.
4. Have them try one themselves. Give hints rather than answers.
5. Name common mistakes and why they happen.
6. Summarise, then ask them to explain it back in their own words.

## Output
A short lesson in steps, ending with a practice question for them to answer.
