Use this to solve a maths or science problem step by step.

## Steps
1. Restate the problem: what's given (with units), what's asked, and any assumptions.
2. Pick the method or formula, and say why it applies.
3. Work it out one step at a time, keeping the units throughout.
4. Check the answer: are the units right, is the size reasonable, do edge cases make sense, and does it work if you plug it back in?
5. State the final answer clearly, with appropriate significant figures.

## Watch out for
- Skipping algebra steps a learner needs to see.
- If it's homework, explaining the method rather than just giving the answer may be what helps most. Ask if you're unsure.

## Output
The worked solution, with the final answer in bold.
