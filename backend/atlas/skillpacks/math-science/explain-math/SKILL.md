Use this to explain a maths idea clearly.

## Steps
1. Start with intuition: what the idea means and why anyone cares, with a picture or analogy.
2. Give the formal definition, with every symbol explained.
3. Work through a simple example step by step, then a slightly harder one.
4. Show where it connects to ideas they already know.
5. Name the common mistakes.
6. Finish with a practice problem and its solution hidden below it.

## Output
The explanation, the examples, and a practice problem. Use LaTeX for formulas.
