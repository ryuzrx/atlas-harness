Use this to write a clear lab report.

## Steps
1. Title and abstract: the aim, the method, the key result and the conclusion, in about 150 words.
2. Introduction: background, the hypothesis and why it matters.
3. Method: materials and procedure, detailed enough that someone could repeat it (past tense, passive or "we" to match the field's convention).
4. Results: tables and figures with units, labels and uncertainty. No interpretation here.
5. Discussion: interpret the results, compare them with the hypothesis and the literature, and cover sources of error and limitations.
6. Conclusion and references.

## Output
The report in those sections, with placeholders for any data you don't have.
