Use this to explain and apply statistics honestly.

## Steps
1. Clarify the question and the type of data (categorical or numeric, paired or independent, sample size).
2. Describe the data first: centre, spread and shape, with a plot.
3. Pick the method and check its assumptions (normality, independence, equal variance). Use non-parametric alternatives if the assumptions fail.
4. Report effect sizes and confidence intervals, not just p-values. Explain what they mean in plain words.
5. Warn about pitfalls: multiple comparisons, p-hacking, correlation versus causation, survivorship and selection bias, and small samples.

## Output
The method, the results with interpretation, the assumptions you checked, and the caveats, plus code if asked.
