Use this to write a focused cover letter.

## Steps
1. Read the job posting. Pull out the top 3 requirements and the company's mission or a recent piece of news.
2. Opening: the role, plus a specific hook showing why this company.
3. Body: 2 or 3 short examples (situation → action → measurable result) that match the requirements.
4. Close: what you'd bring in the first months, and a confident call to action.
5. Keep it under about 300 words, in a warm but professional tone, matched to the company's culture.

## Watch out for
- Repeating the resume.
- Generic praise of the company.

## Output
The letter, with placeholders for anything you'd have to guess.
