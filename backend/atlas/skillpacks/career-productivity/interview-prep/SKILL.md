Use this to prepare for a job interview.

## Steps
1. Research the company: product, customers, news, values and the interviewers if they're known.
2. Map the job requirements to 5 to 7 stories in STAR format (situation, task, action, result), each with numbers.
3. Prepare the common questions: tell me about yourself, why this role, strengths and weaknesses, conflict, failure, and questions specific to the role.
4. For technical roles, practise the likely exercises out loud.
5. Prepare 3 to 5 thoughtful questions to ask them.
6. Do a mock interview: ask questions one at a time and give feedback.

## Output
A prep sheet: company notes, stories, likely questions with answer outlines, and your questions.
