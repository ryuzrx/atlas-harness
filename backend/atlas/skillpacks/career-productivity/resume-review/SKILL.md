Use this to improve a resume.

## Steps
1. Check that it targets the role: a clear headline, and skills and keywords from the posting.
2. Rewrite bullets as action verb + what you did + measurable result ("Cut load time 40% by…").
3. Put the most relevant and recent experience first. Remove old or irrelevant detail. One page if under about 10 years of experience.
4. Formatting: simple and readable by applicant-tracking systems (no tables or images for key content), consistent dates and tense.
5. Proofread for typos and inconsistencies.

## Output
The top 5 changes, rewritten bullets (before → after), and the revised resume if asked.
