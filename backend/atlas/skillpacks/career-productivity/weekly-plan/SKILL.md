Use this to plan a productive week.

## Steps
1. Review: last week's unfinished items, upcoming deadlines and fixed meetings.
2. Pick the 3 most important outcomes for the week.
3. Block focus time for them, early in the day and early in the week.
4. Batch small tasks and email into set slots. Leave buffer time (about 20%).
5. Plan a short daily check-in and a Friday review.

## Output
The 3 outcomes, a day-by-day plan with time blocks, and a "not this week" list.
