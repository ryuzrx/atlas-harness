Use this to summarise a long chat or thread.

## Steps
1. Find the topic and who's involved.
2. Pull out the decisions, the agreed facts, the action items (who and when), and open questions.
3. Note any disagreements and where they ended up.
4. Keep exact figures, names, links and dates.
5. Leave out greetings, repetition and side discussions unless they matter.

## Output
TL;DR (2 or 3 lines), decisions, action items, open questions, and key details.
