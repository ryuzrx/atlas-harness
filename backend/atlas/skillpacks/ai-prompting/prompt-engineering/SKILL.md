Use this to write a clear, reliable prompt.

## Steps
1. State the task, the audience and the goal up front. Give context the model can't guess.
2. Be specific about the output: format, length, structure, and what to include or leave out.
3. Give 1 to 3 good examples if the format or style matters. Make them varied.
4. Separate the instructions from the data, using tags or delimiters.
5. Ask the model to reason before answering on hard tasks, and to say when it doesn't know.
6. Test it on several inputs, including edge cases, and change one thing at a time.

## Watch out for
- Vague words like "good" or "detailed" without saying what they mean.
- Piling on instructions in all caps instead of explaining why something matters.

## Output
The prompt, a short note on why it's built that way, and test inputs.
